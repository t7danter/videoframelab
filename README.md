# VideoFrameLab

[Open VideoFrameLab](https://videoframelab.com/)

VideoFrameLab is a free browser-based tool for extracting still images from local videos. You can preview and capture frames without uploading your video or creating an account.

## How to extract frames

1. Choose a video from your device or drag it into the workspace.
2. Capture a single frame, extract frames at regular intervals, or specify how many frames to collect.
3. Review the results and download PNG or JPG images. You can also export a ZIP, a contact sheet, or a PDF storyboard.
